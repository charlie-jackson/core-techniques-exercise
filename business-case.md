# UrbanEats

## Business Case

### 1. Executive Summary

UrbanEats current success means the company is now looking to expand its operations into new cities. Out of three cities analysed, we recommend opting to expand into City B. Having consulted with regulatory bodies, their regulatory requirements align with our values, and the demographic of the population should ensure continued growth.

### 2. Proposed Solution

We have conducted research into three potential cities to determine where the strengths and weaknesses lie in each option (see decision-matrix). We recommend expanding UrbanEats into City B, which has a large population, low unemployment, high income levels (resulting in higher levels of disposable income) and high smartphone uptake (critical in terms of access to our service).

### 3. Approach

We conducted a Stakeholder Analysis, a Risk Analysis and created a Decision Matrix to drill into the potential pitfalls, bottlenecks and preferred solution available. We have looked at three cities with differing demographics in order to inform our chosen decision.

### 4. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

We have identified the primary risks for our expansion as being high competition, driver availability and restaurant participation. We plan to mitigate against high competition by sticking with our values and mission statement of offering the cheapest service available. Low unemployment rates in our chosen city should mean that there are drivers available to deliver our orders, but unemployment levels are high enough to recruit more drivers as necessary. A good marketing strategy & hosting focus groups with restaurants should ensure high participation by restaurants.

### 5. Value Analysis

City B wins across the board in terms of value to this project. Higher income levels means higher disposible income (for spending on restaurant food); higher population means more potential customers; number of restaurants means more potential for revenue growth; low average time for competitors should ensure customer satisfaction which will ultimately result in repeat business.

### 6. Milestones

1. Research & decision on proposed location
2. Onboarding restaurants
3. Reaching out to potential stakeholders
4. Advertise for jobs
5. Delivering the service
6. Frequent re-evaluation & monitoring for opportunities
