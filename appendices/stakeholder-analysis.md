### Stakeholder Engagement Strategies

1. **Management Team:**
    - UrbanEats management team need to be kept updated with the project every step of the way.
    - Engagement strategies could include weekly project updates including restaurants signing up for the service in the new city, and early demonstrations of the product in the new city.
2. **Customers:**
    - Customers in the new city would need to know UrbanEats is now available in their area.
    - Engagement strategies would include a multi-channel marketing strategy, offers/discounts on release to encourage uptake, focus groups to determine what customers want to see from a food delivery service, and inbuilt feedback capabilities to provide instant feedback.
3. **Delivery Drivers:**
    - Need to be kept abreast of services being offered in their local area.
    - Engagement strategies would include hosting focus groups to determine what they want to see from the service, and a marketing strategy to make drivers aware that our service is now available in their area.
4. **Local Restaurants:**
    - Local Restaurants need to know our service is now available in their area. 
    - Engagement strategies could include hosting focus groups to obtain feedback on the proposed launch of our service in their city, questionnaires to specify metrics in terms of what they would like to see from our service, and a comprehensive marketing strategy to make them aware that we now operate in their area.
5. **City Officials & Regulators:**
    - City Officials and Regulators play a crucial role in ensuring we are operating in line with regulations and city requirements.
    - Engagement strategies could include regular meetings to update them on the operation of our business and for them to update us on requirements as they change, and a one-off meeting to explain fully our company's policies and procedures in order to gain their reassurance.
